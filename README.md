## Documentation Guidelines

Every practical lab and investigation should include:

1. **Objective:** What am I trying to learn or investigate?
2. **Environment:** Tools, operating systems, and lab setup.
3. **Methodology:** Steps and commands performed.
4. **Evidence:** Screenshots, logs, and relevant outputs.
5. **Analysis:** Findings and their security implications.
6. **Conclusion:** What I learned and what can be improved.

## Learning Principles

- Learn through hands-on practice.
- Document every lab and investigation.
- Understand the reason behind every finding.
- Practice detection, analysis, and incident response.
- Continuously improve cybersecurity knowledge and skills.
